B2B FAQ

Private Label FAQ

What information is needed for a private label grow plug quote?

Share the destination market and sales channel, crop or application, preferred model or tray fit, packaging format, estimated volume and shipping destination.

Can product and packaging samples be approved before a bulk order?

Yes. Buyers can confirm the grow plug, packaging format, artwork inputs and carton details before bulk production is discussed.

Which private label packaging formats can be reviewed?

Retail boxes, refill pouches, display packs and bulk cartons can be reviewed according to the selected product, channel and project scope.

How are label claims and market requirements handled?

MCCS can coordinate accurate product information and available supporting documents. The buyer or importer remains responsible for final label approval and local market requirements.

Can export documents be coordinated with packaging plans?

Yes. Available export documents, carton markings, buyer labels and shipping method can be reviewed after the project details are confirmed.
