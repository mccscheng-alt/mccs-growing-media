Cutting Propagation: Root Observations and a Nursery Trial Checklist

By MCCS Growing Media · September 24, 2026

These original photographs were supplied by MCCS from its participation as an exhibitor in Yunnan. They show cuttings grown in molded substrate plugs. Visible roots are useful to inspect, but a purchasing decision also needs a trial with your crop, tray and growing conditions.

Three views of rooted cuttings

What can we see?

Pale roots are visible along the outside of the plugs and below their bases. The photographed plugs retain a recognizable outline. These are observations at the time of photography, not measurements of rooting speed, strength or transplant survival.

What the photographs do not establish

The record does not document crop varieties, rooting dates, irrigation conditions, plug models or a control group. It cannot establish earlier rooting, absence of root circling, zero shedding or reduced transplant shock. A photograph also cannot measure air-filled porosity or EC.

A practical nursery evaluation

  1. Confirm the crop, cutting type and tray geometry before selecting a model. Compare the approved catalog dimensions with your actual tray.
  2. Use comparable cuttings and a current-substrate control. Label batches and record the number started, treatments, temperature, humidity and irrigation.
  3. At agreed observation dates, record rooting counts and photograph the sides and base. Note moisture, root distribution and losses instead of judging only the most attractive specimen.
  4. Check plug removal and handling under your actual moisture conditions. Record breakage or loose material rather than assuming that a cohesive photo means no shedding.
  5. Continue observations after transplanting. Record plant establishment and losses over the same period for each group before deciding on repeat supply.

Questions from propagation buyers

Do these photos prove faster rooting?

No. A documented timeline and a comparable control are needed to assess rooting speed.

Can we identify the model from the photo?

No. Confirm the tray dimensions and compare the catalog; do not estimate plug size from a hand.
