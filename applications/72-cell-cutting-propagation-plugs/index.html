Procurement takeaway

A tray described as “72-cell” is not a complete dimensional specification. Confirm cavity opening, usable depth, taper and drainage before selecting samples, then approve the plug through a controlled side-by-side trial.

Measure the tray before selecting a plug

Cell count describes tray capacity, not the geometry of each cavity. Two 72-cell trays can use different openings, depths, tapers and bottom-drain designs. Send a tray drawing or measurements taken at clearly marked points.

  • Record top opening length and width or diameter.
  • Measure usable cavity depth and bottom opening.
  • Note taper, support ledges, inserts and drainage restrictions.

Define the cutting propagation workflow

Tell the supplier which plant material is being rooted, how cuttings are inserted, how trays are irrigated and how rooted plugs are removed or transplanted. These operating details help distinguish a geometric fit from a workable production fit.

  • Identify crop or cultivar and cutting stage.
  • Record manual or automated loading and removal.
  • Describe irrigation, drainage and environmental controls.

Run a controlled side-by-side trial

Compare candidate plugs with the current medium in the same greenhouse zone. Keep plant material, irrigation inputs, nutrition and observation timing aligned. Do not treat an isolated plant or a single successful pickup as proof of repeatable performance.

  • Label every treatment and retain the sample reference.
  • Photograph dry fit, hydrated fit and root-zone condition.
  • Record rejects and handling observations using defined categories.

Separate measurable results from operating context

Record plug hydration, seating, drainage clearance, stability during handling and root-zone observations, but keep environmental and crop variables beside those results. This makes the trial useful for purchasing, growing and operations teams without turning one trial into a universal performance claim.

Prepare the sample and import brief

Before dispatch, confirm sample count, destination, courier account, importer contact and destination-country document requirements. MCCS can coordinate available commercial invoice, packing list, SDS, composition and test-report support after the product and route are confirmed.

Buyer FAQ

72-cell propagation questions

Does every 72-cell tray use the same plug size?

No. Cell count does not define cavity opening, depth, taper or drainage geometry. Provide tray measurements or a tray drawing before model selection.

How should a 72-cell cutting plug sample be tested?

Run it beside the current medium using the same tray, cultivar, cutting stage, irrigation inputs and greenhouse zone, then record fit, hydration, handling and root-zone observations.

What information is needed before sample dispatch?

Provide cavity measurements, crop or cultivar, current medium, preferred sample count, delivery destination and any courier account or import-document requirements.

Can MCCS guarantee rooting results from the tray format alone?

No. Rooting response also depends on plant material, environment, irrigation, nutrition and operating practice. Approval should be based on a controlled buyer trial.
