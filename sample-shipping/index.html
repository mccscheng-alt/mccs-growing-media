Sample and destination details

  • Crop, application and propagation stage

International Clearance

Confirm the Import Route Before Dispatch

Carrier acceptance and destination import approval are separate checks. Confirm both before the sample is collected.

Buyer or importer confirms

  • Destination-country import and customs requirements
  • Importer contact, broker and permit needs if applicable
  • Required certificate wording or additional declarations
  • Preferred courier account and clearance instructions

MCCS coordinates

  • Accurate product description and declared sample purpose
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Available SDS, composition and test-report support
  • Outbound inspection documents when required and available

Route check: Documentation depends on product composition, destination and importer requirements. Available documents should be reviewed before dispatch; no carrier route or certificate should be assumed to guarantee customs clearance.

B2B FAQ

Sample & Shipping FAQ

What information is needed for grow plug sample logistics?

Share the crop and application, target market, preferred model or tray fit, sample count, packaging needs, shipping destination and courier account if available.

Can MCCS help compare models before shipping samples?

Yes. Buyers can review the catalog first and contact sales for model guidance before confirming a courier sample.

Can export documents be discussed before bulk orders?

Yes. Export coordination, carton discussion and available document support can be reviewed after sample and project details are confirmed.

Who confirms destination-country import requirements for an international sample?

The buyer or importer should confirm destination import and customs requirements with its broker or relevant authority. MCCS can provide available product and export documents and coordinate dispatch after the route is confirmed.
