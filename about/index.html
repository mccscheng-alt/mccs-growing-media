About MCCS Growing Media

MCCS supports overseas buyers through a Guangzhou-based supply chain for molded coconut coir and peat substrate plugs, packaging coordination, export-ready order management and research-informed controlled-environment trial support.

Company

MCCS Growing Media is an export-facing B2B brand operated by Guangzhou Chengfeng Trading Co., Ltd., with production supplied through an authorized manufacturing partner in Guangzhou.

Facility

Authorized manufacturing support with up to 100,000 individual molded substrate units per day under full-line operation; actual output depends on model, packaging and schedule.

Testing

Manufacturing-partner SGS SVHC screening, MSDS support and published Agronomy 2025 PFAL research context are available for qualified project review. The SGS conclusion applies to its tested sample.

Markets

Target markets include North America, Saudi Arabia, the wider Middle East, Europe and Asia-Pacific distributors.

About MCCS FAQ

How does MCCS work with the factory?

MCCS is operated by Guangzhou Chengfeng Trading Co., Ltd. as the authorized export-facing project channel for a Guangzhou manufacturing partner. MCCS coordinates samples, commercial communication, packaging and export workflows.

Can buyers request testing information?

Current SGS SVHC screening information, MSDS support and applicable project documents can be shared with qualified B2B buyers. Source files are not published for unrestricted download.

Can MCCS support overseas sourcing workflows?

Yes. MCCS can coordinate sample review, packaging discussion, order communication and export shipping details for qualified projects.
