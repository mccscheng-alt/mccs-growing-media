Buyer FAQ

Saudi & Middle East Buyer FAQ

Is MCCS suitable for Saudi greenhouse trials?

MCCS molded coir and peat grow plugs can be sampled for Saudi and Middle East greenhouse, PFAL and nursery trials. Buyers should test hydration behavior, tray fit, root-zone handling and local irrigation conditions before bulk ordering.

What should be tested before bulk ordering?

Recommended sample checks include wet-size stability, root containment after transplanting, tray or cup fit, irrigation response, packaging condition after courier delivery and crop-specific performance under the buyer's own growing conditions.

Can MCCS provide research references and documents?

For eligible B2B projects, MCCS can share SGS report support, batch testing information and research references such as the 2025 Agronomy PFAL study on molded coconut coir substrate under its published test conditions.

Can packaging be prepared for Gulf distributors?

Bulk cartons, retail boxes, refill pouches, instruction cards and carton markings can be discussed after model selection, sample approval and buyer project review.
